Introduction:

What are we building?


We are building a method of externalization of will, suppose a scenario where there is a hyper motivated students who has all sorts of dreams like completing a whole 100 lecture playlist on Data Structures And Algorithms, maybe even 
the entirety of Stanford's Autumn 2018 course of ML instructed by Dr. Andrew Ng, but the student may do the first chunk of lectures fast but may struggle later on because of things like missed goals, diminishing motivation, being distracted and so on.


Our solution is called a lecture loaner, the theory behind is that humans unless given an external motivation to do a work may not do so, even if they may know that in the long term its good for them, but most humans just like Models, 
like you have a short context window and may slowly begin to forget why are they even doing the said thing of completing an entire playlist of Data Structures and Algorithms as fast as possible, my proposal of external motivation is a threat!

The idea:

The basic idea of the lecture loaner is that every night at 11 pm, you go to the lecture loaner application, where you manually select specific lectures you want to watch within the next 24 hours, what do I mean by selecting a lecture?
Think like this you click on a button named select lectures, there a pop up window comes up, where you can browse through windows files and you can only select .mp4 videos, and videos will be selected one by one, after selecting the videos, the list of videos will appear in front of you, which works as a verifier to make sure that you are sure you want to target these .mp4 files/videos/lectures only within the next 24 hours, then you click on a button named start session, and then after clicking a start session button, a thank you page appears, which has dazzling and provocative colors and really stands out but is not an animation but only a static page with motion of no sorts.

Token system:

Now you may ask, how will the lecture loaner will make sure that that I only use the strict video player only to watch the lectures. For that I want that after watch the lecture, then before clicking the one "Close App" button on the top left corner of the screen, a pop up will appear where a random hash will appear, and I will simply copy it and maybe paste it on a notepad or anywhere, it should be a hash so that it can't be predicted by me and then fool the system. So like within 24 hours that is 11 pm to 11 pm I selected 10 videos to watch, I watch the 10 lectures using only the strict video player only, where at the end I get a pop up with a random hash generated within it which are called tokens, and then at 11 pm, the lecture loaner appears, stating that my time is up and will tell me click on a button named "Submit Tokens". Then after clicking on the "Submit Tokens" button, a box will appear where I can enter individual tokens in any order, does not matter whether I enter the last, then first and then the third token, no order of submission of tokens does not matters, what matters is that I have submitted all the tokens and then click on a button below the box that reads as "Done" to confirm my entering of the tokens. 

Now to create a sense of urgency, when its 11 pm and I am asked to click on the "Submit Tokens" button, while I don't click the "Submit Tokens" buttons, a really nasty alarm will play at the background, this is the alarm I am talking about: "C:\Users\shiny\Music\COME_ON_MAN_COME_ON_ITS_TIME_TO_WAKE_UP.mp3", when I click on the "Submit Tokens" button, the alarm will be quiet for 40 seconds so that I can peacefully enter the tokens in the box and click on the "Done" button and finally the session ends. But within the 40 seconds, if I did not enter all the tokens then I will be directed back to the previous page where again the nasty alarm will be playing and I'd have to click on the "Submit Tokens" button again, and then again enter the tokens. What you have to do is to make sure that the tokens I receive at the end of the lecture are matched by Lecture Loaner, for that maybe you can create a database where when a session is initiated then for the selected lectures, tokens are generated and hen stored in the database, and then the when watching one of the selected lecture, the relevant token for the selected lecture appears at the end of the lecture for me to collect that is, copy it, and also keep the database encrypted so that I can't just read the database and illegally get the tokens.


Shutdown:

If at 11 pm where Lecture loaner comes at me screaming with the alarm urging me to submit the tokens, and me out of annoyance were to shut down my laptop, then make it sure that the next time I start my laptop again at any time, the lecture loaner will come again telling me to enter the tokens, that is make lecture loaner persistent so that even if I were to shut down the laptop for like 3 hours and then boot it up again, the lecture loaner will remember that I have not entered the tokens, now you see how you'll do it.
